About

Hi, I'm Mekdim.

I bring a blend that's rare in marketing: brand and communications experience alongside real technical skills. I've spent five years on brand and communications for Aerospace New Zealand, Esri Australia and ChristchurchNZ, working across graphic design, campaign material and video, with a focus on event promotion and delivery.

On the technical side, I have a background in aircraft engineering, CAD, prototyping and additive manufacturing, which I use to bring concepts to life. That means I understand technical products and can explain them clearly. I'm open to freelance marketing, brand and design contracts.
